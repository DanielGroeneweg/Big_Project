I have heard the stories of the He-mennekes ever since I was little. It was a cautionary tale; if you misbehave, you’ll end up one of them.
They are mischievous, cruel, and are known to cause chaos. Stories go, that they drowned someone once.
Never in my life did I think that I would have to stand face to face with them, but they crossed a line when they just... took my brother.
Earlier tonight, I heard commotion in his room. The noise... It was them. I KNOW it was them.
I have to get him back.
